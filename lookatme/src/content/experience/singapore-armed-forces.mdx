## Reporting for a unit, not a spreadsheet

Training administration for a unit runs on recurring reports: who is current on which competency, who is due, where the fitness numbers sit. Done manually, the work is re-entry, and re-entry is where the errors live.

I built a **Power BI dashboard** covering training competencies and fitness metrics for **100+ soldiers**, designed for commanders rather than for clerks — so that performance gaps and training-resource allocation could be read off the screen instead of reconstructed from spreadsheets.

Recurring reporting was automated and standardised with **Excel and VBA**, cutting manual data entry and making the output consistent between reporting periods.

I received a **commendation from my unit's Commanding Officer** for the dashboard and analytics work.
