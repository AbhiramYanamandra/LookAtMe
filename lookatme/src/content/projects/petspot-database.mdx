## Overview

PETspot is a marketplace with several distinct kinds of participant, which is what makes it a worthwhile modelling exercise rather than a CRUD table. The data model covers **buyers, sellers, pets, services, enquiries and breeds**, and the relationships between them are where the design decisions live — a seller offering services is not the same relationship as a seller listing a pet, and an enquiry can point at either.

## Normalisation

Functional dependencies were derived from the model and the relations carried through **first, second and third normal form**, producing a schema with redundancy removed and update anomalies designed out rather than handled in application code.
