## Overview

A custom-built nine-key macropad: a 3×3 grid of mechanical switches with per-key RGB lighting and programmable layers, so each key can do different things depending on the active layer.

## What the render shows

The image on this page is a **PCB render**, not a photograph of a finished unit. Visible in the render:

- a 3×3 grid of mechanical switch footprints;
- a small display module on a four-pin header labelled GND, VCC, SCL, and SDA (an I²C connection);
- a six-pin header beside the display;
- four mounting holes at the board corners.
